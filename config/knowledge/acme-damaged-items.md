# Damaged items

## Reporting a damaged item

If an item arrives damaged, tell us within 7 days of the delivery date. We ask for
the order number so we can look up what was delivered and when. You do not need to
send a photo, and you do not need to return the damaged item before we act.

## Eligibility for a replacement

Replacements are available for 7 days from delivery. The 7 days are counted from
the delivery date recorded on the order, not from the order date and not from the
date you contact us. An order that has not been delivered yet cannot be reported
as damaged, because the item has not reached you.

A replacement is only possible when all of the following are true:

- the order status is delivered
- the delivery was 7 days ago or less
- the item category allows replacements
- no replacement has already been created for that order

## Items that cannot be replaced

Gift cards, perishable goods and digital products cannot be replaced. If one of
these arrives damaged or unusable, a colleague will look at the case individually.

## One replacement per order

Each order can have one replacement. If a replacement already exists for an order,
we do not create a second one automatically. A person reviews the case instead.

## High value replacements

A replacement worth INR 5,000 or more is checked by a person before it is sent.
This is a routine check, not a sign that anything is wrong with the request.

## What happens next

Once a replacement is created you get a replacement reference beginning with REP.
The replacement usually arrives within the standard delivery window for your area.
