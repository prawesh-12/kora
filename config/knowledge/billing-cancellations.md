# How a cancellation works

A subscription can be cancelled in two ways: it can stop at the end of the
period the customer has already paid for, or it can stop immediately.

Stopping at the end of the period is the usual choice. The customer keeps
access until the date their current period ends, and that date is stated back
to them.

# When a person has to look first

Cancelling a subscription that is unpaid waits for a colleague to review it.

# What the customer is told

A cancellation is only described as scheduled or done once it has been read
back from Stripe and confirmed.
