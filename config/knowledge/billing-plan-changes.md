# How a plan change works

A plan change moves the subscription on to a different price. Moving mid-cycle
usually produces a proration: a credit for the time already paid for on the old
price, and a charge for the rest of the period on the new one.

The proration is quoted from Stripe before the change is made, and the change
is checked afterwards against that quote.

# When a person has to look first

A large mid-cycle proration credit waits for a colleague to review it.

# What the customer is told

The new plan and the proration are only described once the subscription has
been read back from Stripe on the new price.
